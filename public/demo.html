Click the button below to start voice-powered form filling

Instructions:

  1. Click the "Start Voice Form Filling" button
  2. Allow microphone access when prompted
  3. Speak your answers clearly when asked
  4. Press Escape key to stop at any time
  5. The SDK will fill the form automatically

Language Support:

You can switch languages programmatically:

Aawazz.setLanguage('hi-IN'); // Switch to Hindi
Aawazz.setLanguage('en-IN'); // Switch to English
